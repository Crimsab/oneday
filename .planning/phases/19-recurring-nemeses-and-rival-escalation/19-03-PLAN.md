# Plan 19.3: Nemesis Dossiers and Escalation Traceability

## Goal

Surface nemesis history, status, and escalation traces through codex/dossier tooling without leaking hidden schemes.

## Tasks

1. Define what the player should be able to inspect about a nemesis: last seen, visible scars, known grudges, suspected agenda, and escalation footprint.
2. Link nemesis state into codex, world reactions, fronts, and related NPC/place entries.
3. Keep hidden plans hidden while still showing enough continuity for the nemesis to feel like a persistent actor.
4. Add browse/test coverage for partially known and fully revealed nemesis states.

## Verification

- nemesis continuity is inspectable through dossiers/codex without spoiling hidden plans
- players can trace why a rival matters now
- partially revealed nemeses remain understandable rather than opaque
