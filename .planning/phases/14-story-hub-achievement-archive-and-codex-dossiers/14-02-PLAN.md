# Plan 14.2: Character Dossiers and Inspectable Sheets

## Goal

Turn the current character inspection experience into a clearer dossier system so the protagonist and other inspectable characters can be understood through structured, player-facing data instead of one long monolithic overlay.

## Tasks

1. Define a player-visible dossier view model for the protagonist and inspectable characters, combining stats, vitals, traits, titles, relationships, achievements, and recent story relevance without exposing hidden NPC-only state.
2. Refactor the current `/stats` presentation into a more structured inspection flow that can support focused sections, richer summaries, and cross-links into related story entities.
3. Add a way to inspect relevant characters from story context, including protagonist data and public NPC dossier data such as role, disposition/relationship axes, known goals, and recent involvement.
4. Ensure private thoughts, hidden desires, and other narrator-only internals remain redacted even when dossiers become more descriptive and interconnected.
5. Add focused tests for dossier data extraction, redaction rules, missing-field fallbacks, and navigation into related views.

## Verification

- the protagonist sheet reads like a usable dossier rather than a raw text dump
- inspectable character data is available from story context without leaking private NPC internals
- relationship and achievement information becomes easier to read without flattening everything into one wall of text
- dossiers degrade gracefully when a story has sparse NPC or progression data
