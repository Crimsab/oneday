# Plan 19.4: Resolution Paths Beyond Simple Death

## Goal

Give nemesis arcs multiple meaningful resolutions so the system supports stories richer than “kill the rival and move on”.

## Tasks

1. Define supported nemesis outcomes such as capture, truce, humiliation, alliance, exile, succession, or death.
2. Ensure each outcome leaves canonical fallout in fronts, relationships, world reactions, and future encounter generation.
3. Prevent hardcoded one-size-fits-all resolution by validating AI-proposed outcomes against the nemesis state machine.
4. Preserve room for unresolved or transforming rivalries instead of forcing immediate closure.

## Verification

- nemesis arcs can end or transform in multiple coherent ways
- chosen resolutions leave persistent fallout in the wider world-state
- the runtime does not collapse every rivalry into a combat-only death check
