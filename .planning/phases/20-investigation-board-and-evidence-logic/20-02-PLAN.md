# Plan 20.2: Evidence Normalization and Theory Movement

## Goal

Let the AI propose clue and theory updates while the engine normalizes them into safe canonical board changes.

## Tasks

1. Define the structured AI contract for adding, revising, linking, and discrediting evidence items.
2. Normalize AI-proposed board updates into canonical entities and reject malformed or continuity-breaking mutations.
3. Support theory movement such as strengthening suspicion, revealing contradictions, or collapsing a bad lead without making the board feel like a rigid quest checklist.
4. Add tests for duplicate clues, contradictory updates, hidden-to-known transitions, and malformed evidence payloads.

## Verification

- evidence updates are engine-normalized rather than blindly accepted from AI output
- theory movement remains descriptive and continuity-oriented
- malformed or contradictory updates degrade safely
