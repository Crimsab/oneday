# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players continuing long-running AI-driven role-playing stories in the browser.
They need to read comfortably, choose or write an action, and inspect canonical
world information without losing their place in the narrative.

## Product Purpose

OneDay is a narrative player for persistent, branching stories. Success means
the story remains the primary surface while choices, free actions, challenges,
maps, audio, history, and advanced controls remain available without competing
with the current scene.

## Brand Personality

Atmospheric, restrained, trustworthy. The interface should feel focused and
quiet enough for extended reading while still making consequential actions and
live state unmistakable.

## Anti-references

- Warm parchment, brass-heavy, or faux-editorial styling.
- Dense administration dashboards that expose every tool at once.
- Neon gaming launchers, decorative glows, and ornamental motion.
- Interfaces that use generated art as a substitute for canonical game state.

## Design Principles

1. Keep narrative, choices, and the action composer in the primary reading flow.
2. Reveal tools on demand and preserve the player's reading position.
3. Use one restrained accent for action, selection, focus, and real state only.
4. Keep generated media subordinate to canonical text and structured state.
5. Prefer familiar controls, clear hierarchy, and predictable keyboard behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operation throughout. Preserve visible
focus, reduced-motion behavior, screen-reader labels, responsive layouts, and
timing-free challenge mechanics. Color must never be the only state signal.
