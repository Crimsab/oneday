# Plan 21.3: Project Surfaces and Lasting Outcomes

## Goal

Surface project status and make completed projects produce durable narrative and mechanical consequences.

## Tasks

1. Expose active and completed projects through dossiers, codex, downtime flows, or other appropriate surfaces.
2. Define the lasting outcomes projects can grant, such as new capabilities, stronger relationships, crafted lines, rituals, or base improvements.
3. Ensure completed projects feed into fronts, social systems, crafting, and future story context rather than feeling siloed.
4. Add verification for active-project browsing, completed-project fallout, and partial-progress visibility.

## Verification

- the player can inspect project progress and outcomes cleanly
- completed projects leave durable world-state or capability changes
- project systems integrate with the rest of the game instead of remaining decorative
