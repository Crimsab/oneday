# Plan 19.2: Nemesis Reuse in Encounter Generation

## Goal

Make active nemeses return coherently through encounter generation instead of feeling like random re-rolls of similar NPCs.

## Tasks

1. Extend encounter/retrieval logic so active nemeses can be preferred or reintroduced through fronts, world reactions, and local pressure.
2. Reuse remembered player patterns, past outcomes, and scars when generating future scenes, tactics, and dialogue.
3. Prevent overuse by defining pacing and visibility rules for when a nemesis can or should reappear.
4. Keep re-entry contextual so the system supports direct revenge, proxy action, rumors, or political fallout.

## Verification

- recurring nemeses reuse canonical history instead of respawning as generic lookalikes
- reappearances are paced and contextual rather than spammy
- nemesis scenes feel linked to past events in a way the player can recognize
