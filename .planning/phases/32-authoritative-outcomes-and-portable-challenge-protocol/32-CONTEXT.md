# Phase 32 Context — Authoritative Outcomes and Portable Challenge Protocol

OneDay currently lets narration happen before mechanical resolution and reduces
challenge results to pass/fail. This phase moves resolution ahead of narration,
defines one versioned JSON contract for every host, persists it with branch and
commit lineage, and retains deterministic adapters for old boolean callers.

The engine owns degree, roll, margin, consequence budget, costs, deltas,
revealed facts, and pressure. The narrator may describe those facts but may not
upgrade the degree or award unsupported benefits. TUI, browser gateway,
autoplay, fixtures, and replay exchange the same serialized contract.
