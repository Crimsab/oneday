# Plan 21.2: Opportunity Cost and World Pressure

## Goal

Make downtime choices meaningful by tying project advancement to fronts, pressure, resources, and fail-forward consequences.

## Tasks

1. Define the costs and tradeoffs of spending time on projects while fronts and regional pressure continue to evolve.
2. Integrate project advancement with time passage, risk, resources, and possible setbacks instead of treating progress as free.
3. Allow failures or interruptions to create new hooks, debts, complications, or pressure rather than simply deleting progress.
4. Keep downtime strategic without making it feel punitive or spreadsheet-heavy.

## Verification

- downtime progress has real opportunity cost against systemic world pressure
- setbacks create continuity-rich consequences instead of nullifying the system
- project choices feel meaningful but not oppressive
