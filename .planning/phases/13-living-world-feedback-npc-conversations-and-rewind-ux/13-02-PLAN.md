# Plan 13.2: Nearby NPC Conversations and World Reactivity

## Goal

Deepen the social layer so nearby NPCs feel conversationally available, relationships become multidimensional, and the world reacts visibly to what the player does.

## Tasks

1. Extend NPC persistence beyond `disposition` to richer relationship axes such as trust, fear, debt, respect, and intimacy/tension, with migration-safe defaults.
2. Design and implement a nearby-NPC conversation flow (`/talk` or equivalent) that scopes candidates intelligently to the current scene and preserves canonical transcript continuity.
3. Add intent-aware talk actions such as asking, probing, bonding, bargaining, threatening, promising, lying, or confessing, and map them into relationship/world updates.
4. Add a world-reaction feed for rumors, local heat, faction stance shifts, notoriety, and deferred consequences that emerge from prior turns.
5. Fold fail-forward design into consequence handling so failures create costs, complications, or social/world fallout instead of hard stops.
6. Add targeted tests for relationship persistence, nearby-NPC selection, and world-reaction continuity.

## Verification

- the player can enter a dedicated nearby-NPC conversation flow without dumping the whole world roster into the UI
- relationship axes persist and evolve independently rather than collapsing into a single scalar
- failures produce visible downstream consequences instead of dead-end blockage
- rumors/heat/faction responses can surface after the causative event and still feel connected
