# Plan 18.2: Narrator Contract for High-Stakes Dialogue

## Goal

Let the AI frame stakes, tone, and consequences for social duels without bypassing engine authority over the mechanics.

## Tasks

1. Define the structured AI contract for triggering, describing, and continuing a social duel.
2. Separate AI-authored scene framing from engine-owned duel state and resolution fields.
3. Ensure the AI can propose stakes, leverage opportunities, and fallout while the runtime validates and normalizes them.
4. Add fallback behavior for providers or prompts that only partially honor the social-duel contract.

## Verification

- the AI can set up compelling high-stakes scenes without directly deciding the duel winner
- duel metadata remains validatable and safe when partially missing
- prompt/runtime contracts stay aligned instead of drifting into prose-only improvisation
