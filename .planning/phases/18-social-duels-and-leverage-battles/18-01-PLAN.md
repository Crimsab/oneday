# Plan 18.1: Social Duel State Machine

## Goal

Create an engine-resolved social-duel system for high-stakes dialogue using explicit round state, stance, leverage, and objectives.

## Tasks

1. Define which narrative situations escalate into social duels and what state they need: goal, stakes, patience/composure, leverage, tempo, and round ownership.
2. Build the action vocabulary for the player and NPC, such as appeal, pressure, deceive, concede, expose, withdraw, or escalate.
3. Resolve duel outcomes using engine-side formulas on top of stats, skills, relationship axes, and situational modifiers instead of letting the AI arbitrate success.
4. Keep duels compatible with fail-forward outcomes so losing creates consequences rather than a dead-end refusal.

## Verification

- social duels have explicit engine state and do not rely on invisible prompt-only mechanics
- duel outcomes are engine-resolved from known inputs
- loss states remain playable and continuity-friendly
