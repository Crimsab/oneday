# Plan 13.1: Turn Delta and Hook Tracking

## Goal

Make the game easier to follow turn by turn by surfacing meaningful consequences and keeping unresolved narrative threads visible without turning them into rigid quest objectives.

## Tasks

1. Define a structured turn-delta model that can combine engine-derived changes and optional AI-authored summaries into a single `What changed this turn?` payload.
2. Persist and render clear deltas for stat changes, inventory gains/losses, relationship movement, lore discoveries, world-state updates, and other meaningful turn consequences.
3. Add a hook tracker for promises, debts, mysteries, timers, rumors, and active unresolved threads, with canonical persistence and resume-safe reconstruction.
4. Integrate turn-delta and hook-tracker output into the narrative UI and save/load flow without overwhelming the main prose.
5. Add focused tests for delta extraction, hook persistence, and resume/reload reconstruction.

## Verification

- each turn can produce a readable structured delta summary instead of forcing the player to infer everything from prose
- hooks survive save/load and resume coherently
- the tracker remains descriptive and continuity-focused rather than prescriptive or railroading
- missing or partial AI metadata degrades safely to engine-derived summaries
