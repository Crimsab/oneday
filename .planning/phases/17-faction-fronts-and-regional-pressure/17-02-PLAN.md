# Plan 17.2: Regional Pressure and Heat Integration

## Goal

Fold local heat/reputation into faction fronts as regional pressure instead of creating a disconnected second system.

## Tasks

1. Define how towns, regions, and factions express pressure, suspicion, hostility, influence, or instability through the front model.
2. Map failures, noise, violence, reputation swings, and narrator-driven world changes into regional pressure updates.
3. Ensure pressure changes can affect prices, access, patrols, reactions, rumors, and encounter likelihood without requiring hardcoded quest logic.
4. Keep the model descriptive and systemic rather than purely punitive.

## Verification

- “heat” and “reputation” are represented through regional pressure instead of a parallel orphan system
- pressure has visible systemic fallout in the world
- pressure changes remain interpretable to the player and reusable by other systems
