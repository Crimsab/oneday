# Plan 17.3: Fronts in Hooks, World Reactions, and Context

## Goal

Make fronts materially affect continuity by wiring them into hook tracking, world reactions, fail-forward fallout, and AI context-building.

## Tasks

1. Connect front changes to unresolved hooks, timers, rumors, and existing world-reaction feeds so escalation is visible in the same continuity layer.
2. Feed discovered front pressure and stakes into context-building so the AI writes toward the same world-state the engine is tracking.
3. Use fail-forward and setback flows to advance fronts organically instead of requiring only explicit faction events.
4. Ensure front-driven consequences remain local and comprehensible instead of becoming omnipresent background noise.

## Verification

- front progress changes what hooks and world reactions the player sees
- AI context reflects discovered front state instead of inventing parallel escalation
- fail-forward outcomes can advance the world without railroading the player
