# Plan 15.3: Failure Surfacing and Deterministic Recovery

## Goal

Stop silently swallowing critical persistence failures and make interrupted-turn recovery deterministic on resume/load.

## Tasks

1. Identify all critical persistence errors currently ignored in narrator/session/save paths and replace silent failure with explicit propagation, structured logging, or a visible degraded runtime state.
2. Define how startup, resume, and load detect interrupted or partially mirrored turns and choose the canonical recovery source without mixing future state back into the run.
3. Add repair/backfill behavior where it is safe, but keep the recovery rule simple: reconstruct from authoritative canonical data instead of guessing from partially written mirrors.
4. Tighten save/load and rollback behavior so interrupted writes, superseded autosaves, and resumed sessions never produce ambiguous turn ownership.

## Verification

- critical canonical persistence failures are no longer silently ignored
- resume/load after an interrupted write reconstructs a deterministic canonical snapshot
- recovery does not leak future turns or half-committed state back into the story
