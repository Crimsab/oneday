# Plan 14.1: Home Archive and Story-Scoped Achievements

## Goal

Let the player browse stories and their unlocked achievements from the home surface, while also upgrading the in-story achievements flow from a flat text overlay to a structured story-scoped browser.

## Tasks

1. Define the home-surface archive entry point and the data needed per story, including achievement counts, recent activity, and enough metadata to identify the run without loading it.
2. Add storage/query helpers for per-story achievement summaries and story-level archive metadata without forcing full story hydration for every browse action.
3. Build a story archive interaction where stories are listed first, each story can expand accordion-style to reveal unlocked achievements, and selecting an achievement opens a focused detail view with description and supporting metadata.
4. Upgrade the in-story `/achievements` flow to reuse the same structured browser concepts while staying filtered to the currently loaded story only.
5. Add coverage for empty states, archived stories, zero-achievement stories, story filtering/selection, and achievement detail rendering.

## Verification

- the player can browse per-story achievements from the home surface without loading into the run first
- home-surface achievement browsing respects story boundaries instead of mixing achievements from different runs together
- the in-story achievements surface shows only the current story and supports inspecting individual entries cleanly
- empty states and archived-story flows remain understandable instead of feeling broken
