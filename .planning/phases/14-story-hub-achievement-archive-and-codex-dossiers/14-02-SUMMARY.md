# Summary 14.2: Character Dossiers and Inspectable Sheets

Replaced the old monolithic `/stats` wall of text with a dossier-driven inspection flow backed by canonical story data. The protagonist now opens as a structured dossier instead of a plain overlay, and a new `/characters` command lets the player browse the people index for the current story.

The dossier model surfaces vitals, attributes, secondary stats, skills, traits, titles, achievements, recent chapter relevance, and known people links while staying on the player-safe side of NPC privacy. This gives character inspection a cleaner information architecture and a better foundation for future story-context inspection.
