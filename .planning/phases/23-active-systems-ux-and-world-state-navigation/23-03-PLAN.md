# Plan 23.3: Front Tracker and Fallout Surface

## Goal

Expose discovered fronts, regional pressure, and world fallout through a dedicated tracker so the living-world layer feels active instead of buried in summaries and codex backlinks.

## Tasks

1. Design a front tracker view for discovered fronts, pressure hotspots, and recent fallout.
2. Surface progress, stakes, status, pressure severity, and resolution state without leaking hidden front data.
3. Connect front pressure and world-reaction fallout so players can see why the world is changing, not just that it changed.
4. Add coverage for no-front, rumored-front, known-front, and resolved-front flows.

## Verification

- discovered fronts are easy to inspect without leaving the main play loop
- pressure and fallout are readable as systemic state, not scattered prose
- hidden fronts remain protected while visible fronts feel alive
