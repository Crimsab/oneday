# Plan 17.4: Front Visibility in Codex and Journals

## Goal

Surface discovered fronts and their fallout through codex, journals, and dossiers without spoiling hidden faction plans.

## Tasks

1. Define the browseable representations for fronts, regional pressure, and fallout in codex/journal surfaces.
2. Show what the protagonist knows: current pressure, known factions, seen consequences, and outstanding threats, while hiding internal engine-only fields.
3. Link fronts to affected places, NPCs, factions, and hooks so the player can understand why the world is changing.
4. Add coverage for empty-state, hidden-state, and partially discovered front flows.

## Verification

- the player can inspect discovered front state without seeing hidden plans
- front fallout is traceable through codex/journal links instead of prose alone
- partially discovered fronts degrade cleanly instead of feeling broken or over-explained
