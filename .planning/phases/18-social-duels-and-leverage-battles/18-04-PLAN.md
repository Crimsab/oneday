# Plan 18.4: Leverage, Aftermath, and World-State Consequences

## Goal

Persist the consequences of social duels into relationships, fronts, and world reactions so they matter after the scene ends.

## Tasks

1. Define how leverage is gained, spent, exposed, or lost across a duel and how much of it remains persistent afterward.
2. Feed duel outcomes into relationship axes, debts, suspicions, pressure, rumors, and faction fallout where appropriate.
3. Add readable aftermath summaries so the player understands what changed because of the duel.
4. Ensure recurring NPCs and future systems can reuse duel history as canonical state.

## Verification

- social duel outcomes leave persistent world-state traces instead of evaporating after narration
- relationship and world-reaction systems can consume duel aftermath coherently
- the player can understand why a duel mattered after it ends
