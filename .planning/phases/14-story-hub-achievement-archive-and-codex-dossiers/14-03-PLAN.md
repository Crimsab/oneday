# Plan 14.3: Descriptive Codex, Dialogue Normalization, and Multi-Instance Navigation

## Goal

Add a codex that gathers canonical story knowledge into richly defined entries, fix the remaining narrative dialogue normalization issues, and let the player drill through linked entities using new inspector instances instead of accordion-only expansion.

## Tasks

1. Define the codex taxonomy and canonical sources for entry types such as people, places, factions, mysteries, and active threads, plus the minimum metadata each entry needs to feel grounded and navigable.
2. Build a codex indexing/materialization layer that merges story state, NPC data, chapter summaries, hooks, world reactions, achievements, and canonical history into stable entry records and cross-links.
3. Fix dialogue rendering reliability by normalizing mixed quote styles, deduplicating `dialogue_blocks` against repeated prose more intelligently, and adding a safe fallback that can still surface recognizable spoken lines as dialogue when structured metadata is partial or absent.
4. Implement a keyboard-first drill-down inspector that opens linked entries in new stacked instances or equivalent preserved-context views rather than collapsing everything inside one accordion.
5. Make codex entries highly descriptive and explicitly structured, with sections such as overview, confirmed facts, recent developments, related entities, unresolved questions, and evidence/context for why the entry matters.
6. Add targeted tests for codex linking, duplicate-entity merging, stale-link handling, privacy boundaries, navigation-stack behavior, and dialogue normalization/dedup cases with single-quoted prose.

## Verification

- codex entries feel specific and well-defined instead of vague AI summaries
- direct speech no longer disappears into plain prose just because the narrator used single quotes or repeated the same line outside `dialogue_blocks`
- linked drill-down navigation preserves context better than an accordion-only list
- the codex stays grounded in canonical story state and does not expose hidden NPC information
- players can move across related people, places, factions, and threads without losing their place
